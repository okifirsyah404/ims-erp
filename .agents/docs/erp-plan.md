The integrated package combines the 12 modules in the initial ERP version with six additional features: automated payroll, checkout/payment gateway, WhatsApp AI, automatic licensing API, customer portal, and investor portal.
The workflow ends with the profit-and-loss statement, balance sheet, cash-flow statement, tax working papers, and approved profit-sharing calculations.

PLAN COMPONENTS

Stage 1 • Initial package: 18 tasks; IDR 6,750,000; 20 working days within a 30-calendar-day target.
Stage 2 • Additional features: 14 tasks; IDR 7,750,000; 30 working days, approximately six weeks after Stage 1.
Total development: 32 tasks; IDR 14,500,000. The initial-package cost is charged only once.
Shared contingency: IDR 500,000; used only with the owner's approval.
Total planned budget: IDR 15,000,000, excluding external service costs.

Team and capacity: 2 full-time programmers; 50 productive working days or 100 person-days.
Approximately 10–11 calendar weeks with no additional holidays. Exact dates depend on the start date and holidays entered in Excel.

OVERALL TIMELINE

Implementation basis
The package is for one business entity, one warehouse, a responsive web application, and Indonesian rupiah. The two programmers will use components/templates with which they are already familiar. The owner/finance team must provide data and decisions on time. The price is an internal development allocation for this scope, not a market rate or a guarantee that every possible variation of requirements is covered.

The 30-calendar-day target applies to Stage 1. Additional features are developed in Stage 2. Each module has a clearly defined implementation; basic/manual features that remain in use are described so they are not mistaken for automated functionality.

FINANCIAL CONTROLS AND ACCEPTANCE

Bookkeeping and reports
Opening balances must balance. Down payments received before delivery are recorded as advances according to finance policy; final payment must not duplicate revenue. Journal corrections use reversing entries. The profit-and-loss statement, balance sheet, and cash-flow statement must be traceable to the general ledger; the ending cash balance must agree with the balance sheet.

Tax and profit sharing
Tax types/bases/rates and their effective periods are set by finance at kickoff. Tax output consists of working papers for review, not automatic DJP filing. The profit basis, prior losses, reserves, and shares must be approved by the owner. Tax must not be deducted twice when the basis uses after-tax profit.

Reconciliation
COGS/inventory value, asset depreciation, and manual adjustments must still be checked by finance. Payroll is posted after approval. The gateway distinguishes gross amount, fees, funds awaiting settlement, and cash received. Postings use unique references.

Access and integrations
Internal roles use permissions appropriate to their functions; customers/investors can access only their own information, including through APIs/downloads. API secrets are stored in server configuration. Valid callbacks and idempotency are tested. AI cannot approve discounts or financial transactions.

Acceptance
There are 32 UAT scenarios in Excel: U01–U20 for the initial version and U21–U32 for the additions and integrated workflow. In the final stage, the initial scenarios are tested again. All scenarios must pass and critical findings must be closed before release acceptance.

A task is accepted when its status is “Completed,” progress is 100%, actual start/completion dates are valid, the result is “Accepted,” and the reviewer name and evidence are filled in. Task indicators in Excel help with administration; release decisions still require UAT results, finance reconciliation, and owner approval.

UPDATE ROUTINE
Programmers enter progress and evidence every day. The owner/finance team reviews results regularly. If a task is delayed, revise its local working days and those of dependent tasks, check A/B conflicts, and record the reason. Price-weighted progress is not a measure of profit, invoicing, or money already paid.

Yellow cells are inputs; gray/white cells contain formulas or reference data. Do not overwrite formulas. The Excel file is handed over with status “Not Started” and progress at 0% because there is no evidence of actual work yet. Calendar dates appear after a start date is entered.

HANDOVER DELIVERABLES
